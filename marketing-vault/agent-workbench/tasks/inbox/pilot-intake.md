audience: Pilot decision maker and property-operations sponsor

Create a pilot-ready intake brief from the approved CovePM pilot measurement and pricing context.
Do not invent a company, portfolio, contact, price, discount, price lock, timeline, integration,
or outcome. Where the actual pilot information is missing, create a clear question and mark it as
an approval or discovery item.

The output must cover:

- commercial objective and decision owner;
- current maintenance workflow and operational baseline;
- properties, users, workflows, exclusions, and support assumptions;
- baseline metrics and definitions;
- success criteria and review cadence;
- pricing inputs and approval gates;
- risks, next actions, and kickoff checklist.
