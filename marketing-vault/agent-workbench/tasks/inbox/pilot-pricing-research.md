Use the approved local pricing research and CovePM product context. Produce:

- a comparison of the public pricing patterns, not a claim that competitors are directly equivalent;
- a provisional pilot offer structure;
- a pricing hypothesis for after the pilot;
- the value metrics and cost inputs required to validate willingness to pay;
- questions that must be answered by the pilot before publishing pricing.

Do not set final prices, promise discounts, promise a price lock, or invent ROI. Mark all unresolved
commercial decisions as needs_review.
