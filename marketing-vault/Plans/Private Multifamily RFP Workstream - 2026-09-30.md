# Private Multifamily RFP Workstream

**Started:** 2026-09-30  
**Priority:** Current marketing focus  
**Status:** In progress  
**Scope:** Private multifamily operators; social media deferred. Facebook page remains a separate work in progress.

## Objective

Build a qualified route for Averion Compass to be considered in private-operator technology evaluations and RFPs. Focus first on mid-sized multifamily operators and a bounded inspection-to-repair/unit-turn workflow. This is a hypothesis to validate, not a proven ICP.

## Work assigned

| Workstream | Owner | Deliverable | Completion test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opportunity research | Market intelligence | Dated list of target private operators, verified RFP/vendor-intake routes, source URLs, and fit rationale | Every route is linked to a primary source; closed or unrelated opportunities are labeled clearly |
| Product truth and response readiness | Product marketing | Reusable RFP response outline and claims/evidence matrix for product, implementation, integrations, security, support, and company qualifications | Every claim is backed by approved evidence or marked as an open question; no invented certifications, customer outcomes, integrations, or commitments |
| Exposure and shortlist plan | Demand generation | Decision-maker roles, exposure routes, discovery sequence, metrics, and checkpoint | Plan distinguishes public solicitation from relationship/shortlist cultivation and contains no unsent message represented as sent |
| Workstream coordination | Marketing lead | Consolidated report with priorities, owners, dependencies, blockers, and next decisions | All artifacts linked; external contact, pricing, contractual terms, and public claims remain gated for review |

## Guardrails

- Use **Averion Compass** and **Averion Software LLC** accurately.
- Current evidence supports that teams can record inspection findings, assign follow-up work, and track a unit through review and sign-off. Do not extend this into unverified results, compliance, integration, security, or service claims.
- Research and drafting may proceed. Do not submit forms, contact prospects, publish material, quote pricing, or make commitments as part of this workstream.
- Prepare for private-operator RFPs and qualification processes; do not treat public-sector RFPs as the immediate target.

## Initial success measures

- Number of target operators with verified official routes.
- Number of open or recurring technology evaluation routes relevant to Compass.
- Response-package completeness across required sections, with evidence gaps visible.
- Number of qualified discovery opportunities and invitations to participate in an evaluation (to be recorded only when observed).

## Reporting

Final report should summarize completed research, response readiness, recommended first targets, unresolved evidence gaps, and any action requiring human review. Record observed outcomes separately from hypotheses.
