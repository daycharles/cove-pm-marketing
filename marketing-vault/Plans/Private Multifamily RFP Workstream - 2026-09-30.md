## Objective

Build a qualified route for Averion Compass to be considered in private-operator technology evaluations and RFPs. Focus first on mid-sized multifamily operators and a bounded inspection-to-repair/unit-turn workflow. This is a hypothesis to validate, not a proven ICP.

- Use **Averion Compass** and **Averion Software LLC** accurately.
