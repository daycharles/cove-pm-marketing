## Commercial objective

Test whether a narrow inspection-to-make-ready message earns a qualified discovery conversation with one affordable-housing operator and one regional property-management operator.

No message is approved for sending. These are company-level drafts for human review after the business contact path, suppression check, and channel are approved.

## Shared message discipline

- Lead with one workflow, not the full CovePM platform.
- Ask about current process and baseline rather than promising an outcome.
- Use the evidence-backed narrative: **inspection findings -> accountable make-ready work -> clear ready state**.
- Safe capability language: work assignment, scheduling, bulk actions, timelines, attachments, messages, audit history, and reporting where the release supports the specific workflow.
- Do not claim customer results, integrations, compliance, route optimization, AI outcomes, offline mobile behavior, or fixed implementation timelines.

## Offer and measurement

The initial offer is a 20–30 minute discovery conversation, not a pilot commitment. If discovery confirms a real workflow problem, the next artifact should define one bounded pilot workflow, one baseline, one post-pilot measure, implementation questions, and procurement/security gates.

Primary metric: qualified discovery conversation accepted by an approved business contact.

Secondary metrics: evidence completeness, reply/acceptance rate, workflow urgency, confirmed decision path, and pilot-readiness score.
