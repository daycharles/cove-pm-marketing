# CovePM Marketing OS — Phone Monitoring Runbook

Use this while away from the desk. The private control room is:

https://cove-pm-marketing.daycharles.chatgpt.site/marketing-os

## What runs without you

- Lead and market research can be prepared locally.
- Draft emails, social posts, CRM actions, and demo follow-ups can enter review.
- The hosted inbox refreshes every minute while open and refreshes when the tab becomes active.

## What requires your decision

- Email send, social publishing, CRM mutations, and calendar event creation.
- Pricing, pilot scope, commitments, claims, or anything that creates an external obligation.

## Review loop

1. Open **Approval inbox**.
2. Expand the item and check audience, source, CTA, recipient, and copy.
3. Choose **Changes** if revisions are needed; confirm the prompt.
4. Choose **Approve** when the draft is ready. This does not send it.
5. For approved email, open **Execution queue** and choose **Send via Zoho** only after the final recipient check.
6. Confirm the resulting **Sent** or **Failed** state.

## Daily rhythm

- Morning: review overnight queue and approve/revise the first batch.
- Midday: check new lead research, inbound replies, and scheduled content.
- Afternoon: review the second social batch and any follow-up drafts.
- Evening: confirm no failed sends, unresolved urgent replies, or calendar actions waiting.

## Current pilot boundary

HubSpot is connected for controlled CRM work. Zoho Mail is connected through the approved bridge.
Google Calendar is authenticated but event creation remains manually confirmed. Social publishing
remains approval-gated through the selected scheduler. The system is designed to keep working while
you are monitoring by phone, but it will stop at human gates rather than making unreviewed external
commitments.
