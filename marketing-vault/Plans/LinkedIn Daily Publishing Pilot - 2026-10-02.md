## Operating loop

- Draft one post at a time. Use the on-demand Content specialist for copy when helpful and Review for independent claim/visual QA. Every post gets a distinct central takeaway, a source/claim note, exact caption, selected image and alt text. Use approved [[Brand/Brand Guide]] and [[Strategy/Positioning]]. Do not imply customer results, software features beyond the narrow current positioning, or a live operator pain point without evidence. Keep the editorial mix broader than inspections and maintenance: do not publish two consecutive posts centered on either topic, and target at least four of seven pilot posts on other property-operations themes. Label general operator education separately from claims about Compass.
- **Image pipeline:** First choose a `ready` catalog asset by topic and check [[Assets/social-catalog/usage.csv]] and the future queue. For a new angle, use Hermes `image_generate` for one original square editorial scene. Prompt with fictional adults, credible property setting, navy/brass accents, no text, logos, readable screens or invented UI. Inspect the output at full size for hands, legibility, realism and claim implication; reject defects. Save locally as a new immutable `AC-G###` file, record prompt/provenance/dimensions/restrictions/alt text/status in `catalog.json` and `prompts.json`, and include "Illustrative scene created with AI" in the caption. Do not generate the actual Averion logo or app interface. A generated scene is illustration, not evidence. Host the image at a stable publicly accessible URL before calling Buffer's API; verify response type and bytes, then read back the media attachment in Buffer.
- Reserve the image in `usage.csv` when the exact draft exists; prefer not to repeat any image within 14 days. Run an independent exact-caption/image/source/duplication QA gate before scheduling; store its pass/fail reason and the exact payload for audit. No human per-post approval is requested for this bounded LinkedIn lane. Failed or ambiguous QA means skip the slot and alert the owner, not a filler post. No auto-engagement, outreach, spend, or product commitments.
- Keep a two-day queued buffer where possible, but never exceed Buffer Free's rolling ten-post queue. Only one post per local day; use idempotency tracking and query the live queue before mutation. Read back post ID, dueAt, caption and attachment after scheduling; separately confirm published status and URL later. A queue item is not a published post.
