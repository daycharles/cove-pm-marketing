# Private-operator outreach activation — 2026-10-05

**Status:** Proposed, human-gated first-contact pilot; no email account connected, recipient approved, or message sent.  
**Owner decision needed:** Choose and authorize a company sender, the first exact recipient/message, and a bounded pilot. This plan does not grant bots standing permission to send.

## Objective and evidence

**Target:** Private multifamily operations/maintenance workflow owner and eventual IT evaluator. **Question:** Who owns the inspection-finding → follow-up → unit-review workflow, and what is the appropriate route for a short discovery conversation? This is a hypothesis, not a known operator problem or confirmed buying intent.

It observed Drucker + Falk's generic footer email and relevant IT/maintenance/regional roles, but no validated software-supplier destination. The two-account packet documents **0/2 verified appropriate routes**. The [[Research/Private Operator Product RFP Evidence Request and Gate Matrix - 2026-10-05]] leaves production, security, integration, implementation, pricing and outcomes unverified. The [[Brand/Brand Guide]] and [[Strategy/Positioning]] support narrow general positioning, not a current-release or outcome promise. Do not use rental/SMS, resident, management-client, or credentialing forms as a shortcut.

## First bounded experiment: routing before pitching

1. **Set up a company-controlled sender, not a personal mailbox or unattended credential.** The owner names the email provider, exact `From` identity, reply-to, authorized operator, and approval method. Connect via an approved OAuth/credential mechanism; do not put credentials in this vault, chat, scripts, or Git. Verify access by listing sender identity and Sent/Inbox read-only. No test email to a prospect. Maintain a suppression list, opt-out handling, and message-ID/idempotency record in a restricted store. Start in draft-only mode.
2. **Approve one exact routing inquiry to Winn's published headquarters mailbox**, after the owner confirms this general corporate channel is acceptable for a *routing question*, reviews the exact address/message/sender and company fit, checks suppression and applicable outreach rules, and records approval. This is not a qualified software-evaluation route or a product pitch. If the owner does not accept that channel, do not send; seek a better official route. Keep Drucker + Falk research-only until its supplier/technology route is clarified or separately approved for a similarly bounded routing inquiry.
3. **Only if a recipient confirms a relevant route**, prepare a separate personalized discovery message for exact human approval. Product or implementation claims require product-owner evidence; otherwise ask about the workflow without asserting shipped capability. No attachment, meeting-booking promise, pilot offer, mass sequence or auto-follow-up. A response identifies a route, not buyer interest unless the respondent says so.
4. **Observe and stop.** Check the actual Sent copy and message ID once after a send; if delivery status is uncertain, inspect Sent before retrying. Record any reply verbatim in a restricted account record, honor opt-outs and update suppression before other sends. If no reply, record that fact at the agreed checkpoint rather than claiming rejection or sending an automatic follow-up. Expand to a second account only after a human review of the first exchange and the route/claim QA.

## Measurement and checkpoint

Record accounts researched, routes observed, routes confirmed by an authorized reply, exact drafts approved, sends verified in Sent, deliveries if available, replies, opt-outs, relevant conversations and next action as **separate states**. Baseline for this pilot: zero verified appropriate routes in the two-account source packet and zero sends/replies observed in this pilot; do not import historical workbench approvals as contacts. First checkpoint: owner selects sender/channel and approves or rejects the one-message routing experiment. Second checkpoint: review the actual reply or no-reply status after a human-selected interval; no automated cadence until explicitly authorized.

## Approval and automation boundary

Research, drafting, source QA and reply classification can be bot-assisted. Sending is **per-exact-message human-approved** until the owner separately authorizes a specific bounded automation with limits, suppression, opt-out, idempotency, audit, review and stop controls. The LinkedIn standing exception does not extend to email, DMs, forms, CRM writes, calendar invitations or spend. Do not enable bulk prospecting or autonomous sequences as part of mailbox connection.
