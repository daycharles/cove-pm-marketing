# CovePM Pilot Measurement Plan

Status: working plan for the upcoming pilot. Do not publish as a case study until the pilot data
and customer approval are complete.

## Pilot objective

Test whether CovePM helps a property team move maintenance work from request to completion with
less delay, less coordination effort, and better resident communication.

## Before the pilot

Record the baseline period, portfolio/properties, work-order volume, current workflow, staffing,
vendors, system of record, and any exclusions. Agree on definitions before measuring results.

## Pricing inputs to capture

- Current software subscription and minimums.
- Add-on, payment, screening, communication, and transaction fees.
- Implementation, migration, training, and support effort.
- Staff hours spent triaging, assigning, chasing, and updating work.
- Cost of overdue work, repeat visits, vendor delay, and resident escalation where measurable.
- Portfolio size, property count, work-order volume, and user count.
- Which capabilities are essential versus optional.

## Pilot decision gates

1. Baseline is complete and definitions are accepted.
2. Pilot scope, duration, users, properties, support, and data handling are approved.
3. Success metrics and review cadence are agreed before launch.
4. Results are reviewed with the pilot customer.
5. Conversion pricing is proposed only after value, implementation effort, and required scope are known.

## Claims discipline

Do not publish a percentage improvement, customer result, pricing commitment, discount, price lock,
or case study until it is supported by the pilot record and approved for use.
