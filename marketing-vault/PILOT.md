# Cove Marketing Team Pilot

The current Marketing OS is the first live pilot of the future Cove AI marketing team. It should be operated as a customer-ready service, with internal names and unfinished edges hidden behind a controlled workflow.

## Pilot promise

Run the core marketing department loop for CovePM:

1. Plan campaigns and weekly priorities.
2. Create and approve social and email content.
3. Publish and monitor owned channels.
4. Capture, qualify, and follow up with leads.
5. Answer routine inquiries using approved knowledge.
6. Schedule demos and maintain calendar hygiene.
7. Report activity, pipeline movement, and lessons learned.

## Productization rules

- Every recurring task must have an owner, trigger, input, output, approval rule, and success metric.
- Every customer-facing message must be attributable to a workflow or agent role.
- Store reusable prompts, playbooks, templates, and escalation rules separately from one-off campaign work.
- Record human interventions; they identify where the future product needs better automation or controls.
- Keep an evidence trail for published content, sent emails, replies, booked meetings, and outcomes.

## Human approval gates

Require approval before publishing new campaign themes, sending high-volume outbound email, making pricing or product commitments, responding to complaints, or handling legal, privacy, security, or reputational issues. Routine approved workflows may run without approval.

## Pilot scorecard

Track weekly:

- qualified leads generated
- lead response time
- follow-up completion rate
- demos booked, held, and missed
- email delivery, open, click, and reply rates
- social output and meaningful engagement
- hours of human work required
- automation failure or escalation count
- pipeline or revenue influenced

## Weekly pilot review

For each workflow, decide whether it is:

- repeatable and ready to package
- useful but still requiring human review
- unreliable and needing redesign
- not valuable enough to keep

The pilot is successful when the system can produce measurable pipeline with decreasing human effort, while maintaining brand quality and safe escalation behavior.
