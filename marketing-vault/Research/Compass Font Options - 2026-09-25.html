What to compare with Michael

The app stylesheet reviewed uses a system sans-serif stack for general text and loads Nunito for headings, navigation, buttons, labels, and badges. Nunito’s own project describes it as primarily a display family, though usable for text. A single UI family may make the interface feel more even and less ornamental.

  • Choose Inter if you want the safest, most neutral product UI.
  • Choose IBM Plex Sans if you want clear utility with a little more technical character.
  • Choose Source Sans 3 if you want the calmest, most understated reading feel.
  • Choose Manrope if you want to keep the geometric Averion personality while reducing the sci-fi effect.

My shortlist: compare Inter against Manrope first; keep IBM Plex Sans as the technical alternative. In any selection, use regular/medium weights for data, keep labels at normal letter spacing, and avoid loading a thin weight for small text. A font change alone cannot fix browser zoom, display scaling, or antialiasing.

Font loading note: samples use Google Fonts. If the page is opened offline, the system fallback may display instead.
