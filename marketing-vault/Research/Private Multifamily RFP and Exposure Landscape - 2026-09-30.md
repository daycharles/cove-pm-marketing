## Executive readout

Private multifamily software procurement is mostly relationship-led and not exposed through public bid boards. In the official operator sources reviewed, **Greystar is the clearest published route for consideration in an upcoming RFP**. Most other public “vendor” portals reviewed concern trade contractors, property-level service providers, insurance/compliance, invoicing, or catalogs; they are not evidence of an enterprise software RFP route.

For Compass, the most credible near-term path is company-level discovery with operations/maintenance and strategic technology owners, asking to be routed into the relevant evaluation process. Treat any RFP as an opportunity to qualify, not an assumption that the operator is sourcing this category. This aligns with Compass’s currently evidenced wedge: recording inspection findings, assigning follow-up work, and tracking a unit through review and sign-off. Do not claim unverified integrations, security certifications, scale, customer outcomes, or implementation capabilities.

## Recommended sequencing (research recommendation, not outreach authorization)

1. Prepare a truthful one-page software overview and a short workflow demo around inspection finding → assigned follow-up → review/sign-off. State evidence gaps plainly.
2. Validate implementation, data handling/security, access controls, supported devices, integrations, service/support, pricing, references, and pilot measurement before answering any RFP. These details are not substantiated by the current positioning note.
3. Start discovery with the more bounded, regionally concentrated hypotheses (Fairfield Homes, MG Properties, WinnCompanies, RPM Living, Weidner, and Gables). Ask who owns property operations/maintenance workflow and technology evaluation. These are proposed targets, not confirmed leads.
4. In parallel, test Greystar’s published RFP consideration form for whether it accepts property-operations software submissions. Keep Bell and BH vendor registration as conditional service-vendor routes only; do not confuse compliance enrollment with product evaluation.
5. Use NAA Supplier Partnership/Operator Circle or Apartmentalize only after defining target personas and validating the exposure economics. Membership/exhibiting is awareness and access, not evidence of a buying cycle.

## Limits and verification notes

- No operator was contacted; no form was submitted; no account or subscription was created.
- No live private operator software RFP was verified in this review. Public information supports a potential RFP consideration route at Greystar, not a currently open solicitation.
- Several “vendor” forms are expressly for contractors, goods/services, credentialing, work orders, catalogs, or payment administration. They must not be represented internally as SaaS procurement paths.
- Operator target fit is inferred from portfolio scale, geographic concentration, property-management focus, and visible operations/technology functions. It is not evidence of an active need, budget, procurement timetable, or product compatibility.
- Accessed 2026-09-30. Operator pages and procurement processes can change; recheck before acting.
