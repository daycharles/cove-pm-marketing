# Averion Software Brand Guide

## Brand structure

- **Averion Software** is the company brand. Use **Averion Software LLC** for legal identity contexts.
- **Averion Compass** is the property-management product for inspections and unit turns.
- **StellaAI by Averion Software** is a separate automated crypto-trading product. Keep it out of
  Compass messaging unless a piece is specifically about StellaAI.
- Use **Averion Compass** in all current marketing, sales, and product materials. Do not use retired
  product names in new copy; preserve them only in dated source records or technical compatibility identifiers.

## Logo and visuals

- Use the existing Averion logo asset from the website repository. The logo wordmark reads
  **Averion**; do not add “Software” to the logo itself.
- Keep the approved silver mark and gold stroke. The website palette pairs those with navy, white,
  and light gray. Use gold as an accent and preserve strong contrast.
- Use the current website screenshot set in `Assets/website-screenshots/` as the visual reference.
  Do not redraw, stretch, recolor, or replace the logo.

## Voice and claims

- Write like a person: short, clear sentences and specific descriptions of the work.
- Avoid generic AI claims, empty superlatives, and language that promises speed, savings, or results
  without measured evidence.
- Current public Compass positioning: teams can record inspection findings, assign follow-up work,
  and track a unit through review and sign-off.
- Keep Compass and StellaAI as separate products with separate audiences and jobs.
