Cove Property Management · Product brief

Property-management UI competitive research

Design direction for an efficient operations cockpit that builds on competitor strengths without becoming a generic record-management application.

Prepared and accessed: 2026-09-13 · Research artifact committed as 28d0fa7
Recommendation: organize Cove around portfolio context + operational queues + next actions. Make Needs attention the operator’s home base, preserve context through drilldowns, and make every repeated workflow batchable and auditable.

What Cove should borrow—and improve

AppFolio

Borrow: the Unit Turn Board and cross-module continuity between maintenance and leasing.

Improve: keep the human owner, conversation, and next action visible in one context.

Buildium

Borrow: unified lifecycle coverage, recurring maintenance, portals, and onboarding support.

Improve: put the daily queue above feature modules and make tier/automation boundaries explicit.

Yardi Breeze / Voyager

Borrow: role-based dashboards, centralized data, mobility, and configurable workflows.

Improve: opinionated defaults before customization; avoid a setup-heavy first-run experience.

Entrata

Borrow: record/context/action as the information model and policy-aware automation.

Improve: expose source context, review, permissions, audit, and escalation for every assisted action.

DoorLoop

Borrow: outcome-grouped breadth, one login, mobile, migration, and training.

Improve: prevent feature volume from becoming a long catalog through queue-first workspaces.

Propertyware

Borrow: custom dashboards, reports, open API, and multi-location management.

Improve: allow saved views and safe templates before arbitrary dashboard composition.

Recommended Cove information architecture

Today

Personal queue, Needs attention, recent changes, approvals, and portfolio pulse.

Operations

Work orders, maintenance, inspections, turns, make-ready, preventive maintenance, assets, vendors, compliance.

Leasing

Listings, prospects, applications, screening, leases, renewals, notices, move-in/out stages.

Money

Receivables, payables, payments, reconciliation, approvals, budgets, exception reports.

Residents

Directory, households, communications, portal activity, service requests, lifecycle events.

Portfolio / Insights

Property hierarchy, occupancy, assets, owners, documents, health, saved reports, drilldowns, exports.

Interaction principles: action before record; context travels; exceptions first; one object/many lenses; batch by intent; progressive disclosure; observable automation; role-aware responsive layouts; calm visual hierarchy; accessible by default.

Prioritized implementation map

  1. P0 — Today cockpit: combine / and /attention into grouped attention queues, due/waiting states, and one-click actions.
  2. P0 — Context switcher: extend app-shell.tsx, navigation.ts, and /properties with portfolio/property/building/unit context preserved through drilldowns.
  3. P0 — Command center: expand command-search.tsx and the search API to leases, units, people, communications, and direct navigation.
  4. P0 — Maintenance and turns: compose /work, /work/[id], assets, bulk edit, and assign/notify into a request-to-close flow with readiness blockers.
  5. P1 — Leasing rail: connect listings, leases, and portal into a lead-to-renewal stage view with blockers and batch outreach.
  6. P1 — Money by exception: add a future /money workspace for failed/late payments, approvals, reconciliation, and variance explanations.
  7. P1 — Unified timeline and responsive field mode: reuse work-detail timeline/attachments and styles.css to make related activity and mobile completion first-class.
  8. P2 — Role templates and automation observability: provide useful defaults and preview/audit/rollback affordances in settings.

Acceptance criteria for the next UI pass

  • A manager identifies and opens the five highest-priority items from the first screen without visiting a module index.
  • Work detail links property, building, unit, resident, lease, asset, vendor, communication, and audit history while preserving the originating queue.
  • Search works with Ctrl/Cmd+K and /; queues support composable URL filters, multi-select, preview, and result summary.
  • At 320px, 768px, and 1440px, critical controls remain visible, keyboardable, labelled, and not color-only.
  • Task testing targets under 30 seconds from “needs attention” to next action for work, leasing, and payment exceptions.
  • Playwright, accessibility, and visual regression coverage includes Today, attention, properties, listings, leases, work detail, portal, and settings at desktop/mobile widths.
  • No reverted logo asset or branding is introduced.
